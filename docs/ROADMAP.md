# Roadmap

What Bhook does today and what's coming. Live progress is on the [project board](https://github.com/users/PrataapMS/projects/4); each item below links to its issues through the board.

## Shipped

| Area | What you get |
|---|---|
| Foundation | Installable PWA, serverless API, demo mode with no keys, key checker, tests |
| Search and ranking | Location or typed area; cuisine, meal, budget, diet and distance; ranking by trustworthy rating, relevance, distance and budget fit; directions |
| Review summaries | Verdict, what to order, downsides, red flags and trend for the top places; choose the model; re-rank by review sentiment |
| Menus and prices | Menu on tap from the restaurant's site or the web, with source and date on every price |

## Next

| Milestone | What you'll get |
|---|---|
| **v1.0 Launch** | Verified against real data, protected from abuse, deployed and installable |
| **Personalisation** | Bhook remembers your visits: a quick 👍/👎 after you go, places you disliked drop out, cuisines you love get a nudge, and there's always one place you haven't tried |
| **Polish** | Lunch-time nudge, share a pick with friends, last results available offline |

## Not planned

- Ordering or delivery
- Accounts or social features
- Storing Google's place data long-term

Have an idea? [Open a feature request](https://github.com/PrataapMS/bhook/issues/new/choose).
